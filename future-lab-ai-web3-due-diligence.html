How AI Can Change Web3 Project Due Diligence

How AI code analysis, project identity, evidence and valuation can become one acquisition workflow for Web3 projects.

A Web3 buyer may need to understand code, architecture, dependencies, product maturity, community signals, ownership and business claims before making an acquisition decision.

AI can organize evidence, summarize repositories, identify technical questions and turn large amounts of project information into a structured review. It should assist due diligence, not replace professional judgment or independent verification.

A useful workflow connects technical analysis with project identity and business context. A code audit can answer technical questions; a project passport can organize identity and evidence; valuation can then use the available information as one input.

Web3Market is building this workflow around AI Project Passport, AI Code Audit, AI Valuation and an acquisition-oriented marketplace.

Frequently asked questions

What is AI due diligence for a Web3 project?
AI can organize evidence, analyze repositories and surface technical and business questions for acquisition research.

Can AI replace professional due diligence?
No. AI assists research and analysis; independent verification and professional judgment remain important.

What should a Web3 acquisition review include?
Code, architecture, dependencies, identity, ownership, product maturity, community signals, business claims and valuation inputs.
