## Featured Articles

### AI Agents and Blockchain: Autonomous Commerce
AI agents are moving from generating information toward planning tasks and operating software. Blockchain can provide programmable settlement, wallet-based identity and verifiable transaction history.

### Why Digital Ownership Needs Blockchain
Digital businesses, software, domains and online communities can have real economic value. Blockchain can provide a portable and independently verifiable transaction history for digital assets.

### When AI Becomes an Economic Actor
Autonomous agents create new requirements for identity, permissions, payments and accountability. The interesting frontier is the combination of AI intelligence and programmable trust.

### Will Robots Need Wallets?
As robots become networked and autonomous, machines may need to interact with charging, software and logistics services without human approval for every small transaction.

### How AI Can Change Web3 Project Due Diligence
AI can organize project evidence, summarize repositories and surface technical questions. The stronger model connects identity, code analysis, valuation and transaction records into one acquisition workflow.

### From Websites to Autonomous Digital Businesses
The next generation of digital businesses may include AI agents performing operational work under human governance. This creates new requirements for evaluating code, data, workflows and economic rights.
