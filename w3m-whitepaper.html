1. Executive Summary

Web3Market is a marketplace for buying and selling Web3 projects, websites, applications and digital businesses. Its product architecture combines marketplace discovery with AI Due Diligence, AI Valuation, Web3 Project Passports, Deal Room workflows, wallet and transaction verification, and blockchain-based settlement.

W3M is planned as the platform's utility token. Its purpose is to provide access, discounts, premium services and ecosystem participation within Web3Market rather than to represent a guaranteed investment return.

2. The Problem

Web3 project acquisitions are fragmented. Buyers often face incomplete information, uncertain valuations, weak verification and disconnected transaction workflows. Sellers also lack a specialized marketplace that presents project technology, evidence and acquisition information in one place.

3. The Solution

  • Marketplace for Web3 projects and digital businesses.
  • AI-powered project Due Diligence and valuation.
  • W3M Project Passport and public evidence pages.
  • Deal Room for structured buyer/seller workflows.
  • Wallet and transaction verification.
  • Multisig-oriented transaction architecture and blockchain settlement.
  • Android access through the Web3Market application.

9. Vesting

  • Team / Founder: 12-month cliff followed by 36-month linear vesting.
  • Advisors: 6-month cliff followed by 18-month vesting.
  • Public presale reference: 25% at TGE and 75% over 6 months, subject to final presale terms.
  • Liquidity: intended lock period of 12–24 months, subject to final launch structure.

10. Roadmap

11. Security & Transparency

The token contract should be independently audited before a public presale whenever funding permits. The project should publish the final contract address, allocation wallets, vesting rules, liquidity-lock information and audit status. No statement such as “audited” should be used until an independent audit has actually been completed.

12. Risks & Disclaimer

W3M is a planned utility token and this document is a technical and business roadmap, not a promise of value or return. Token markets can be highly volatile and users can lose funds. A public token sale may be subject to securities, consumer-protection, AML/KYC, tax and other regulations depending on jurisdiction. Legal and compliance review should be completed before a public sale.
