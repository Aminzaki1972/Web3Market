# W3M V1 — Agreement Article 7 and Transaction Schedule

Status: product-owner-approved draft wording for inclusion in the W3M Standard Acquisition Agreement V1. This document is not the published canonical terms, does not change the current terms version/hash, and is not legal advice or a substitute for local legal review.

## Article 7 — Transaction Consideration and Platform Success Fee

1. The seller determines the asking price. The parties may agree to adjust that price or apply a discount before approving the final transaction consideration.

2. The final transaction consideration means the amount agreed by the buyer and seller after applying any agreed discounts. It must be clearly recorded in the deal data before settlement begins.

3. Web3Market's success fee is 7.5% of the final transaction consideration agreed by the buyer and seller. The fee is deducted from the total consideration paid by the buyer and is not added as an extra charge on top of that consideration.

4. The seller is entitled to the remaining net consideration after deduction of the platform success fee. Settlement follows the approved deal data and uses the currency specified for that deal.

5. Before settlement, both parties must review and approve the final consideration, the platform-fee percentage, and the seller's net proceeds.

6. The final consideration, platform fee, and seller net proceeds are recorded in the deal record so both parties can review and verify them.

7. Any price estimate or valuation range provided by Web3Market tools is non-binding guidance. It does not change the price agreed by the parties or require either party to complete the transaction.

## Transaction Schedule — Deal-specific data

The transaction schedule records the actual values for the individual deal and contains these fields:

- Seller asking price.
- Agreed discount, if any.
- Final agreed transaction consideration.
- Transaction currency.
- Platform success-fee rate.
- Calculated platform-fee amount.
- Seller net proceeds.
- Buyer approval status and timestamp.
- Seller approval status and timestamp.

The platform fee is calculated as 7.5% of the final agreed transaction consideration. Seller net proceeds equal the final agreed transaction consideration less the platform fee. Both calculations use the deal's currency and the canonical decimal-precision and rounding rules.

No illustrative dollar values, sample transaction amounts, or hypothetical payout figures are included in this agreement wording. Actual amounts belong only to the relevant deal's transaction data.
