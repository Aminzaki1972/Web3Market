AI Agents and Blockchain: Autonomous Commerce

Explore how AI agents could use wallets, smart contracts and verifiable blockchain transactions to participate in autonomous commerce.

Home / Future Lab / AI Agents and Blockchain: Autonomous Commerce

AI agents are moving from generating information toward planning tasks and operating software. When an agent needs to pay for a service, receive a digital asset or prove that an action occurred, intelligence alone is not enough; it needs economic infrastructure.

Blockchain can provide programmable settlement, wallet-based identity and a verifiable transaction history. Smart contracts can enforce predefined rules, while human governance can define spending limits and actions that require approval.

For Web3 businesses, this creates a potential path from AI-assisted operations to bounded autonomous commerce: discover an opportunity, evaluate it, execute an approved transaction and preserve evidence of what happened.

Web3Market's model follows the same broader direction: AI can analyze and organize evidence, while blockchain can provide a verifiable settlement layer for digital ownership and transactions.
