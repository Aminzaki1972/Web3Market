Settlement

A payout/refund must be an authorized Safe transaction, preferably one atomic batch. A database status change is not a Safe lock. Dispute protection requires a separately reviewed Safe Guard / Module design.

Dispute center

Dispute statusNo dispute

Dispute procedure

  1. Claimant submits a reason and supporting evidence.
  2. Other party is notified and receives the contractually defined response period.
  3. Web3Market reviews both parties' evidence and issues a reasoned written decision.
  4. Decision specifies seller payout, buyer refund or partial allocation under the signed agreement.
  5. Authorized Safe transaction is prepared and executed; on-chain result is recorded.

No real dispute record is created.

A platform decision is a contractual internal dispute mechanism, not a court judgment. Legal review and explicit response deadlines are still required.
