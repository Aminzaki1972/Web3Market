1 · Submit a dispute request

Either buyer or seller may request administrative review. Submission immediately suspends new payout instructions in this demo, but does not itself open a formal dispute or confirm a Safe lock.

2 · Administrative review

The administration reviews the request and records a reasoned approval or decline. These controls simulate an administrator; they do not authenticate a real admin.

3 · Respondent response and evidence

This is a local simulation. Safe lock is not verified.

The respondent may reply within the agreed response window.

Platform fee · 7.5% of original grossCalculated from agreed price
The buyer receives the buyer gross refund bucket minus the full platform fee. The buyer gross bucket plus seller payout must equal the original deposited gross amount. Amounts below the full fee or allocations that do not balance are rejected.

A decision is unavailable until the response phase is complete and a demo deposit has been recorded.
