W3M Protocol

Evidence-Driven Acquisition Verification and Conditional Settlement Protocol. A protocol being developed to connect verifiable transaction evidence with strict authorization conditions before a digital acquisition can proceed to blockchain settlement.

CORE ARCHITECTURE DEVELOPED · END-TO-END INTEGRATION & VALIDATION IN PROGRESS

Why W3M Protocol?

Digital acquisitions involve more than sending a payment. Buyers and sellers need a structured way to validate the project, ownership, agreed terms, asset handover and settlement conditions.

01 · EVIDENCE

Evidence before authorization

Organizes transaction evidence around a specific deal and checks whether required evidence is present, correctly bound and still valid.

02 · FAIL-CLOSED

Conditions cannot be bypassed by a score

Mandatory requirements remain mandatory. An AI assessment cannot compensate for missing or invalid evidence.

03 · FRESHNESS

Revalidation before execution

Evidence and transaction conditions are checked again before settlement authorization so that stale or superseded results do not authorize execution.

04 · SETTLEMENT

Conditional blockchain settlement

Designed to work with Safe multi-signature transaction controls, while keeping custody and execution within the configured wallet flow.

How the protocol evaluates a deal

Evidence is evaluated against the requirements and terms of the specific acquisition.

01

Project identity & ownership

Validate the project identity and evidence that the seller controls the relevant project or asset.

02

Code & asset transfer

Review the relevant code-audit reference and validate evidence of the agreed asset handover.

03

Agreement, payment & acceptance

Bind the deal terms and verify payment and buyer acceptance according to the transaction requirements.

04

Dispute & settlement checks

Check for blocking disputes, validate the settlement transaction and require fresh authorization conditions.

Four explicit decision outcomes

The protocol uses clear outcomes to prevent an incomplete or blocked acquisition from being treated as approved.

PASSAll required conditions satisfied
INCOMPLETERequired evidence is missing
BLOCKEDA blocking condition is present
REVIEW_REQUIREDAdditional review is needed

Development status

We are publishing the development status transparently. The current implementation is isolated from the live marketplace.

Core architecture developed

  • Evidence evaluation and fail-closed decision engine
  • Report integrity and trusted-signer verification components
  • Safe payload, signature and execution validation components
  • Dispute-aware settlement controls and isolated endpoint foundations
  • Component-level automated tests in an isolated development branch

Work still in progress

  • Complete report-issuance runtime and trusted evidence loading
  • Full end-to-end disposable test across the entire transaction lifecycle
  • Provider credential and production-schema compatibility validation
  • Staging review, security assessment and release readiness checks
  • Any future production integration and activation
Important: W3M Protocol is a development initiative and is not currently connected to live Web3Market transactions. No live transaction is represented as protected by this protocol. The described capabilities are subject to completion of integration, validation, security review and a separately approved release.
