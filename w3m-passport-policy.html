W3M Passport Eligibility & Verification Policy

W3M Passport is an evidence-based project identity record. A search result alone does not create or prove a W3M Passport.

1. Research is not a Passport

Any website or Web3 project may be researched. Research results remain non-issued unless the project passes the W3M eligibility rules.

2. Minimum eligibility

  • A canonical project name must be identifiable and must not be a W3M serial.
  • An HTTPS official website must be identified.
  • At least two independent public evidence domains must be available.
  • At least one primary evidence source must be available, such as an official website, GitHub repository, documentation or on-chain contract evidence.
  • The evidence score must reach at least 60/100.
  • The evidence must establish a credible Web3/project identity.

3. W3M identity

A W3M serial is an identifier issued by Web3Market after eligibility checks. The serial itself is never treated as the project's name.

4. Ownership verification

Eligibility and ownership are separate. A project may have an evidence-based W3M Passport without claiming ownership. Ownership verification is a stronger status and requires proof of control of an official project property or supported cryptographic identity.

5. Public SEO

Only eligible verified identities are published as permanent Passport pages and included in the public Passport directory and sitemap. Research-only records are not treated as permanent Passport identities.

6. Reverification

Passport evidence may change. Monitoring can detect material changes and a Passport can be downgraded, suspended or revoked when its evidence no longer supports its status.

7. Transparency

W3M Passport evidence is research-based and should not be interpreted as an investment recommendation, legal certification, financial audit or guarantee of project legitimacy.
