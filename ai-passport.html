W3M Passport Issuance Policy

Being listed on Web3Market does not automatically qualify a project for a verified Passport. W3M distinguishes marketplace approval, project identity verification, and technical assurance. These are future-facing issuance criteria; publishing this policy does not itself run eligibility checks or change any project\u2019s status.

Marketplace status

1. Listed — Not Verified

A project may be eligible for marketplace listing after the marketplace's listing review, but it must not be presented as having a verified W3M identity or technical assurance.

Identity checks

2. W3M Identity Verified

Requires a clear project identity, sufficient evidence that the submitter controls or is authorized to represent the project, a reviewable source or product reference, complete core information, and successful basic verification.

  • Project name, purpose, category and technology details
  • Public repository or another verifiable source
  • Ownership/control evidence appropriate to the project
  • Verification date and material limitations disclosed
Additional technical checks

3. W3M Technical Assurance

Requires the identity checks plus technical evidence appropriate to the project's type, maturity and risk, such as reproducible tests, operational checks, and a documented security review with scope, date, findings and limitations.

Minimum eligibility and rejection rules

  • Core identity and project information must be complete and internally consistent.
  • The repository, product, or other declared evidence must be accessible and suitable for verification.
  • For projects claiming to be live web products, a working, independently reachable product URL is expected, unless credible alternative evidence fits the project type. A custom domain is not mandatory when reliable alternatives establish identity and continuity. Evidence of an unstable or untestable product may be insufficient for higher assurance levels.
  • Material ownership doubts, inaccessible evidence, misleading claims, or unresolved critical security findings block the relevant verified status until reviewed.
  • An AI score or automated recommendation alone is never proof of ownership, operational stability, or security.

What a Passport means: it records the identity and evidence W3M has checked; it is not a guarantee of profitability, uninterrupted availability, legal ownership, or vulnerability-free code. Technical audit records are separate and apply only to their stated repository/version and scope. W3M may re-check evidence and suspend a verification status if its basis can no longer be confirmed. Historical identity records may remain visible for sold or inactive projects with their status clearly shown.

Future implementation and validation

  • This page publishes the intended policy only; automatic eligibility enforcement is not activated by this policy text.
  • When the marketplace has expanded, W3M may validate the criteria using projects already listed on the platform, under a separately planned review.
  • Any future automated issuance or rejection logic will be a separate implementation phase, with controlled validation and review before activation.
